Action: add dishes and finish the order through a real channel (phone, official site, app), or get directions to a branch. Constraints: no invented claims; Uzbek Latin; owner-pinned brand yellow #EDCD49 and greens #3E5F21 / #115A2E.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a Karagöz shadow-theatre restaurant: the brand's dark green is the darkened hall, and the food story is played on a lamp-lit yellow screen (perde) by translucent, pin-jointed leather puppets: the cook, the donar spit, the plate, the delivery rider. Turkish food, told in Turkish theatre's own illustrated form. Refuses the painted-poster look of the previous pass (brush-torn edges, watercolour splashes, Young Serif/Caveat) and generic flat food-delivery illustration.
OWN-WORLD: green-950/900 hall and cream paper (#F6F0E1) as the light rooms; the perde as a woven, radially lit yellow screen in a scalloped yellow frame; puppet palette of dyed hide (red #A3241A, tan #C98A4B, rod brown #3A2410) with punched-hole perforations and brass pins; section tops cut as a scalloped valance with punched holes; dish photos (real, official) shown on pale lamp-lit screen tiles (yellow and green woven cloth). Brand yellow #EDCD49 for actions and offers. Yeseva One display (high-contrast swelling serif, single weight), Figtree body. Hand-built SVG only; no generated raster art.
STORY: unchanged — slogan, real dishes with weights and prices, real offers, nearest branch or a real ordering channel.
FIRST VIEWPORT: dark green hall; Yeseva H1 "Ta’mga yangicha yondashuv." with "yangicha" in yellow; copy; yellow "Menyuni ko‘rish", outline "Eng yaqin filial"; free-salad line in yellow Yeseva; on the right the perde: the lamp catches with a flicker, then cook, spit and plate rise on their rods and sway, the cook's knife arm slicing; a cream price ticket for the chicken Yaproq donar with add-to-cart overlaps the screen's foot.
MOTION: one authored moment (lamp catches, puppets rise) on each screen; ambient hand-held sway after; the closing screen (delivery rider) waits until scrolled into view; reduced motion shows the lit, still screen.
